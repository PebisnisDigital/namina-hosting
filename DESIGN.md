# PESONAFLIX Cinematic Identity — design direction

## Identity and purpose

PESONAFLIX introduces a real person through a cinematic personal profile. It should feel like a crafted film-title sequence paired with a tactile collection of identity cards, not an AI-generated SaaS dashboard or generic Netflix clone.

The visitor should immediately recognize the person, understand the story, and be able to explore the profile without learning a complicated interface. Use genuine supplied data; do not invent career achievements, statistics, or profile facts.

## Visual personality

- Mood: editorial, theatrical, confident, restrained, physically grounded.
- Palette: near-black stage and charcoal surfaces; warm ivory typography; muted brass/gold as the single material accent. PESONAFLIX red belongs to its logo, not to every control.
- Typography: oversized condensed display type for the person's name and role, compact but legible sans-serif for profile data. Strong hierarchy is more important than decorative labels.
- Image: one convincingly photographed person on the right. Preserve believable proportions, natural light and skin detail. Keep the face unobstructed.
- Texture: subtle stage lighting, soft film grain, and realistic card highlights. Avoid floating gradients, frosted-glass overload, generic glow or pseudo-luxury ornament.

## Composition

- Desktop: title occupies the upper-left; stacked interactive cards align beneath it; person fills the right side. Maintain a clear separation between foreground cards and the figure.
- No duplicate vertical tab navigation. The cards themselves are the navigation and contain the profile fields.
- The front card must look like a tangible ATM-style card with visible edges of the cards beneath it. Keep content readable when stationary.
- Mobile: reflow the headline and cards vertically, protect touch targets, and avoid horizontal overflow. Do not shrink profile labels until they become hard to read.

## Motion grammar

- Headline: a purposeful automatic stamp-in arrival, not an endless distracting bounce or a replay-button dependency.
- Cards: direct pointer/touch tracking, direction-aware tilt, a short off-screen swipe, and the next card rising from depth. Motion should communicate a physical stack, not a page transition.
- Springs should settle quickly, with no heavy wobble, accidental jumps or layout shifts. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Background character movement is atmospheric support; the content remains understandable when video cannot play.

## Tone and slop checks

- Short Indonesian text with personality and meaning. Remove placeholder marketing hype, unnecessary chapter counters, fake premium claims and ornamental headings that repeat content.
- Every visible badge, line, glow and motion effect must improve identity, hierarchy, orientation or interaction feedback. If it has no purpose, remove it.
- Design read: personal cinematic profile for first-time visitors, with dark editorial typography and tangible cards. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 3.
- Black stage: separates the identity text from the photographed subject and lets the cards retain their material contrast.
- Warm gold accent: signifies the active card edge and directions, where interaction needs a clear affordance.
- Condensed typography: behaves like a film title at large scale and leaves room for legible profile fields.
- One card stack: each chapter is both content and navigation, so no second navigation rail is needed.
- Short stamp-in and swipe depth: show an arrival and directional transfer. Static text does not pulse while being read.
- Treat the current prototype as a starting point to critique, not a design to defend. Verify with real browser interaction, readable mobile layout, keyboard navigation, and honest QA notes.
